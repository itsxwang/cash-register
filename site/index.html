FAQ

Why crypto-only?

The tools here are built for people who already live on-chain — stablecoin freelancers, crypto-native indie hackers. Keeping settlement on-chain means no Stripe/PayPal cutoffs, no account freezes, and prices that don't need to absorb processor fees.

What exactly do I get after paying?

A download link (GitHub Releases) with the tool's files: source, docs, and free updates for the tool you bought. The invoice generator also runs free in your browser — paying gets you the portable version + updates + support.

How is a payment matched to my order?

Every order gets a unique id used as the memo (where the chain supports one) and, on some networks, a unique exact amount. The founder's watchers watch the receiving addresses for those values; a matching transaction unlocks your download.

Refunds?

If a tool doesn't work for you within 7 days, message with your order id — refunds go back to the sending address, on-chain.
