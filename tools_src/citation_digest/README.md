# Citation Monitor — Weekly Digest ($19/month)

Your subscription: every Monday an agent asks AI engines (ChatGPT-class models
via Groq, Gemini) **36 real buyer questions** about your product category and
emails you a digest:

- **Your citation share** — of the 36 answers, how many mention your product
  (tracked week over week).
- **Competitor gaps** — which competitors get cited instead, and in exactly
  which answers.
- **The 3 changes that would flip it** — concrete, ranked fixes based on what
  the engines actually quoted.
- **Quoted sources** — verbatim engine answers with URLs, so every claim is
  checkable.

Sample digest (real engine answers, our demo brand):
https://itsxwang.github.io/citation-monitor/ — baseline week: competitors
cited in 25/36 answers, demo brand at 0/36. That gap is the product.

## What the digest contains

Each Monday's email has, in order: headline numbers (citations you /
competitors, week over week), the 5 most interesting answers verbatim, the
full 36-question table as an HTML attachment, and the ranked "3 changes"
list. Weeks are numbered (ISO week, e.g. 2026-W46) so trends are easy to
follow.
