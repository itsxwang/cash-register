How crypto payment works — in 4 steps

No card needed, no account to create, works from any country. A payment takes about 2 minutes from a phone or desktop wallet.

  1. Pick a chain at checkout.

    We accept ETH (Base), SOL and BTC. Any of them works — pick the one your wallet already holds. ETH on Base and SOL have sub-cent network fees; BTC fees depend on network load.

  2. Send the EXACT amount shown.

    The checkout shows an exact amount like 0.01064 ETH and a QR code your wallet can scan. The tiny unique tail on the amount (and the memo — which is just your order id, like CR-K3M2XQ9F) is how the payment is matched to your order automatically. Scan the QR with your wallet, confirm, done — no memo typing needed when you scan.

    Screenshot (text): wallet app → Scan QR → shows "Send 0.01064 ETH to 0x45DF…d575 (Base)" → Confirm → ✓ sent

  3. Wait ≤ 10 minutes for auto-confirm.

    On-chain watchers (mempool.space / Solana RPC / Base explorers) match your transaction automatically — usually within minutes, worst case ~10 minutes after the network confirms it. You can also email xion.dev7@gmail.com with your order id and the txid to speed things up; the founder confirms manually as backup.

  4. Download unlocks.

    Refresh status.html?order=YOUR-ORDER-ID — when the status turns paid, the download link unlocks on the same page. Save your order id; it's your receipt and your re-download key.

Why crypto?

No card needed, no account, works worldwide. There's no card processor that can decline you, no signup form, and no region locks — if you have a wallet, you can buy. Prices are quoted in USD; you pay the live equivalent in your coin, shown exactly at checkout.

Refund policy

7-day refund, no questions. If a tool doesn't do what you expected, email xion.dev7@gmail.com within 7 days of payment with your order id and you get a refund to your sending address. Downloads aren't locked after a refund — we'd rather you keep it and tell us why it missed.

Support is email-only: xion.dev7@gmail.com — usually same-day.
